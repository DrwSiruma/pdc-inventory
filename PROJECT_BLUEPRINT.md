# Project Name

Inventory Management System

---

# Company

Panda Development Corporation

---

# Purpose

To replace the current Excel-based Inventory Monitoring System used by Accounting, Operations, Warehouse, and Stores with a centralized web-based Inventory Management System.

The system shall provide validation, monitoring, inventory computation, variance generation, audit trail, and reporting while minimizing manual encoding and preventing unauthorized data manipulation.

---

# Core Philosophy

The Inventory System is **validation-based**, not **encoding-based**.

Each department owns its own transactions.

Accounting validates the submitted data.

# Product Beginning

Product Beginning belongs to the Store.

Accounting NEVER encodes Product Beginning.

Product Beginning is created from:

Opening Stocks

*

Verified Ending Inventory of Previous Business Date

---

# Item Beginning

Item Beginning belongs to Accounting.

Accounting maintains item inventory.

---

# Warehouse Deliveries

Warehouse delivers products.

Store receives products.

Store can either:

Approve exact quantity

or

Enter Short Delivery

Once submitted,

Store cannot modify the delivery.

Maximum:

Five (5) deliveries per business day.

---

# Throw Away

Store enters:

Store Throw Away

↓

Accounting physically validates

↓

Accounting enters Actual Throw Away

↓

Verify Throw Away

↓

Official Throw Away becomes part of Inventory Snapshot.

---

# Product Variance

Variance SHALL NOT be generated until:

Inventory is Verified

AND

Throw Away is Verified

Formula

Expected Inventory

=

Beginning Inventory

*

Warehouse Deliveries

*

PDR

*

Verified Throw Away

Variance

=

Ending Inventory

*

Expected Inventory

Variance values become permanent historical records.

# Coding Standards

Use Prepared Statements.

Use Transactions for critical updates.

Create Audit Trail for all critical actions.

Never duplicate business logic.

Keep inventory computations centralized.

Follow role-based validation.

Avoid direct manipulation of submitted records.

# Long-Term Goal

Replace all Excel inventory monitoring with a centralized web-based Inventory Management System capable of supporting all Panda Development Corporation and Dunkin' stores while maintaining inventory accuracy, accountability, auditability, and operational efficiency.
